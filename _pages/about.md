Hi, I'm Rohith! I recently graduated from IIT Madras, and I will be joining the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS) at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, as a PhD student working with [Hai Dang Dau](https://hai-dang-dau.github.io/). I work primarily on sampling algorithms and generative modeling, but I am broadly interested in anything related to applied probability and optimization. I built this site to document my ongoing research journey. You can find a brief overview of my background in the Short CV section, and my published papers and preprints under the Publications tab. The Misc. section is my informal space; right now, it holds my past presentations, but I will soon be using it to post interesting side projects, experiments, and other personal interests.
